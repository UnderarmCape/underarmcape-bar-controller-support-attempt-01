# Radial Mixed-page Presentation

Build and Factory/Lab pages with one category retain the full-circle category background and large heading. Pages with two or more sectors retain colored fills, dividers, and compact sector labels but suppress the redundant combined large heading.

The page indicator is centered at the lower inside edge of the inner panel (`cy - panelRadius * 0.72`). Metadata rendering reserves the page-indicator band, so descriptions, costs, health, damage/range, and warnings do not overwrite it. Native cells and selected-border scaling are unchanged.
