# Controller glyph asset license and source

`controller_glyph_atlas.png` is original generic controller/input artwork created specifically for BAR Controller Companion on 2026-07-20. It was generated from geometric primitives and text by `tools/dev-scripts/Generate_Controller_Glyph_Atlas.ps1`; no Microsoft, Xbox, Sony, Nintendo, Steam, or third-party controller artwork was copied or bundled.

They may also be redistributed under CC0 1.0 where a standalone asset-only license is required.

The face-button colors are metadata tints applied at runtime to original monochrome shapes. Button letters and generic input names are functional labels, not brand artwork.
